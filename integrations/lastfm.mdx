---
title: "Last.fm"
description: "Connect Last.fm for now-playing, charts, crowns, and listening history."
---

## Authorize Last.fm

Run the login command, open Feng's authorization link, and approve it on Last.fm.

```text
,lastfm login
```

## Finish the link

Return to Discord after approval. Feng associates the Last.fm account with the Discord member who started the request.

## Disconnect

Use `,lastfm logout` when you no longer want Feng to use the linked account.
