---
title: "Initial setup"
description: "A safe first-time setup checklist for a new server."
---

## Invite and place Feng

Invite Feng from the [home page](https://feng.up.railway.app/) and move its role above the roles it will assign, remove, mute, or moderate. Feng cannot act on the server owner.

## Check channel access

Feng needs to view the channel, read message history, send messages, and embed links anywhere commands or logs are used. Tickets and VoiceMaster also require channel-management permissions.

## Run a small test

Start with a read-only command, then test one reversible action.

```text
,help whois
,log events
```

Confirm the response, the log entry, and the affected role or channel before staff rely on the setup.
