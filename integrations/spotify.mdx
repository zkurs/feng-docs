## Link your account

Run the login command from your own Discord account and approve access on Spotify. Feng links only the member who started that request.

```text
,spotify login
```

## Playback requirements

Spotify playback controls act on an eligible Spotify device and generally require Spotify Premium. Feng does not stream Spotify audio into a Discord voice channel.

## Disconnect

Use `,spotify logout` to remove Feng's stored encrypted tokens. You can also revoke Feng from Spotify account settings.
