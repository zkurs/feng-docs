## Permission model

Feng checks the command's configured access, the moderator's Discord permissions and role position, then Feng's own Discord permissions and role position.

## Common actions

```text
,ban @member 1d Threatening members
,purge @member 30 #general
```

## Review actions

Use case and moderation-history commands to review recorded actions. Configure a moderation log so staff can audit who acted and why.
