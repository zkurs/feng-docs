---
title: "Tickets"
description: "Create private support channels, staff access, and reliable transcripts."
---

## Requirements

Prepare an open category and staff role. A closed category and transcript channel are optional but recommended.

## Create the system

```text
,ticket setup Open-Tickets @Staff Closed-Tickets #transcripts
,ticket panel send #support
```

## Ticket lifecycle

Staff can claim, rename, move, close, and reopen tickets without exposing the channel to other members.

### Transcripts

Save the transcript before deleting a ticket whenever the conversation may need review later.
