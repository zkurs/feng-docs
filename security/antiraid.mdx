---
title: "Anti-raid"
description: "Configure join-rate protection and account checks."
---

## Inspect configuration

Review the active raid settings and the server's normal join pattern before changing thresholds.

```text
,antiraid config
```

## Choose a response

Use a response proportionate to the signal. A sudden join spike may justify temporary containment, while permanent bans require stronger evidence.

```text
,antiraid configure true 7 true 10 kick false
```

## Verify the gate

After configuration, test with a controlled account and confirm verified members can still join normally.
